Company Overview

Veteran Pushing Forward LLC is a Phoenix-based, U.S. Marine Corps veteran-owned technology services company providing managed IT, cybersecurity, backup and recovery, network infrastructure, systems integration, and private on-premises AI for professional services firms and government contractors. We deliver secure infrastructure support, documentation, and readiness assistance through clearly scoped prime, subcontract, and teaming engagements.

Core Competencies

  • Managed IT — Microsoft 365 administration, endpoint support, patch coordination, remote monitoring, vendor management
  • Cybersecurity — vulnerability reviews, security policies, documented controls, incident response planning
  • Security Setup — MFA, identity hardening, least privilege, firewall/VPN configuration, endpoint protection
  • Private / On-Prem AI — client-controlled document retrieval assistant with approved sources and access controls
  • Network Design — VLAN segmentation, business Wi-Fi, switching, rack integration, cabling diagnostics
  • Systems Integration — server and virtualization deployment, identity integration, configuration documentation
  • Backup & Recovery — backup design and monitoring, restore testing, recovery runbooks, retention reviews
  • CMMC Level 1 Readiness Support — gap check of the 15 FAR 52.204-21 practices, findings report, self-assessment & SPRS affirmation prep
  • CMMC / NIST SP 800-171 Readiness Support — readiness review, remediation planning, segmentation, documentation

Differentiators

  • Veteran leadership: founded and operated by a U.S. Marine Corps veteran (2010–2014, honorable discharge); service-disabled veteran-owned.
  • Security-first delivery: MFA, least privilege, segmentation, logging, and tested backups built into every environment.
  • Client-controlled AI: private AI deployed on approved documents with controlled access — client data never used in public demonstrations.
  • Documented and accountable: written scope, acceptance criteria, change control, and restore-test records.
  • Local presence: Phoenix, Arizona headquarters with secure remote support and on-site installation and assessment capability.

Customers Served & Contracting Approach

Professional services firms (law, accounting, medical and dental offices, insurance, consulting) and government contractors, including small primes and subcontractors. Open to subcontracting, teaming, and micro-purchase opportunities with clearly defined scope and deliverables.

CMMC services are readiness support only; Veteran Pushing Forward LLC does not perform certification assessments.
