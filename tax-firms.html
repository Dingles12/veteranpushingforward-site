No surprises

What happens in week one

  1. Day 1 · Kickoff

    30–45 min call. We confirm scope and name your Qualified Individual.

  2. Day 2 · Inventory

    List of systems, software, and where client data lives.

  3. Day 3 · Checkup

    Remote review of MFA, backups, email, devices, and access.

  4. Day 4 · Risk review

    Findings ranked by priority, in plain English.

  5. Day 5 · Draft

    First WISP draft to you. Week two: your edits, final plan, staff walkthrough.

What you get
