  • Vendor management

Cybersecurity

  • Vulnerability reviews

  • Incident response planning
  • Ongoing managed security

Security Setup

Ways to get started

IT & Security Checkup

Start with a flat-fee IT & Security Checkup. A look at the basics that stop most break-ins and data loss, done mostly remotely, with a written findings report and fixes ranked by priority — in plain English.

  • Multi-factor sign-in
  • Restore-tested backups
  • Old or extra access
  • Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace settings
  • Computer updates & encryption

$300–$500 flat fee, depending on office size. Sign up for a first project or managed plan within 30 days and your Checkup fee is credited toward it.

Founding-client offer (limited): $300 Checkup; the 30-day credit still applies.

WISP & FTC Safeguards Security Plan

Your written security plan, built in plain English. The FTC Safeguards Rule requires tax preparers to keep a written information security plan (WISP). We build it with you, and your firm owns it. Fixing the gaps it finds is quoted separately.

  • For tax preparers & CPA firms
  • Plain-English written plan
  • Annual review available

$1,200–$2,500 · annual review $400–$800

Founding-client offer (limited): $950 for the first 3 tax/CPA firms.

CMMC Level 1 Readiness Support New

Gap check for small DoD subcontractors. A plain-English check of the 15 FAR 52.204-21 / CMMC Level 1 practices, with a written findings report that shows where you stand. We also help you prepare your annual self-assessment and SPRS affirmation.

  • 15-practice gap check
  • Written findings report
  • Self-assessment & SPRS affirmation prep

$300–$500 flat fee

This is readiness support. We don’t guarantee a score or outcome.

About the founder

Meet David Lee Ingles

David Lee Ingles is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran who served from 2010 to 2014 and was honorably discharged. A service-disabled veteran, he founded Veteran Pushing Forward LLC in Phoenix to bring the discipline, accountability, and mission focus of the Marine Corps to the technology challenges facing small businesses and government contractors.

David combines military leadership and technical discipline with systems administration knowledge, networking and security training, and continuing software engineering education — including training at New Horizons Computer Learning Center in Tempe covering Security+ and Network+ foundations, Microsoft systems administration, and Cisco networking fundamentals.

Our commitment: dependable, secure technology with clear scope and accountable support — so you can focus on serving your clients.

Veteran Pushing Forward LLC is owner-operated. Every client works directly with David, from the first conversation through ongoing support.
