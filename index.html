What we do

Managed IT & Network

Cybersecurity & Backup

About the founder

Meet David Lee Ingles

David Lee Ingles served in the U.S. Marine Corps from 2010 to 2014 and was honorably discharged. A service-disabled veteran, he founded Veteran Pushing Forward LLC in Phoenix to bring Marine Corps discipline and accountability to small-business and government-contractor technology. He completed coursework at New Horizons in Tempe in Security+ and Network+ foundations, Microsoft systems administration, and Cisco networking. The company is owner-operated: every client works directly with David.
